03 / Design Direction

Modern Contemporary Aesthetic

Clean lines, floor-to-ceiling glass, flat or low-slope rooflines, and a warm material palette that feels welcoming rather than cold.

Exterior: Butterfly Silhouette

Wings extend from center, flat roof with deep overhangs, stucco and wood accents, floor-to-ceiling glass

Kitchen: Chef's Paradise

9' waterfall island, 48" pro range, custom walnut cabinetry, quartzite counters

Great Room: Indoor-Outdoor Flow

20' ceilings, linear fireplace, multi-panel sliders to L-wrap patio

Primary Bath: Spa Retreat

Freestanding soaking tub, curbless shower with rain head, heated floors

05 / Timeline

Project Phases

A custom home build typically takes 18-24 months from breaking ground. Add 6-12 months for design and permitting. Your 3-5 year timeline is perfect for doing this right.

Phase 1: Vision & Planning

Refine your vision, research locations, interview architects and builders, establish budget parameters

Now - Year 1

Phase 2: Land Acquisition

Find and purchase 2-3 acre lot, conduct soil tests and surveys, understand zoning for multiple structures

Year 1-2

Phase 3: Design Development

Work with architect on detailed plans for all 4 structures, select major finishes, complete construction documents

6-9 months

Phase 4: Permitting

Submit plans for main house + outbuildings, navigate approvals, finalize contractor selection

2-4 months

Phase 5: Construction

Site prep → Foundation → Framing → MEP rough-in → Exterior → Interior finishes → Outbuildings

16-22 months

Phase 6: Move-In

Final inspections, punch list completion, landscaping maturation, move in!

1-2 months
