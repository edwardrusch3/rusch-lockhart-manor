03 / Design Direction

Modern Contemporary Aesthetic

Clean lines, floor-to-ceiling glass, flat or low-slope rooflines, and a warm material palette that feels welcoming rather than cold.

Exterior: Warm Minimalism

Flat roof with deep overhangs, stucco and wood accents, floor-to-ceiling glass walls that blur indoor/outdoor lines

Kitchen: Chef's Paradise

10' waterfall island, 48" pro range, custom walnut cabinetry, quartzite counters, butler's pantry

Great Room: Gathering Space

20' ceilings, linear fireplace, multi-panel sliding glass walls, polished concrete or wide-plank oak floors

Primary Bath: Spa Retreat

Freestanding soaking tub, curbless shower with rain head, heated floors, floating double vanity

05 / Timeline

Project Phases

A custom home build typically takes 18-24 months from breaking ground. Add 6-12 months for design and permitting. Your 3-5 year timeline is perfect for doing this right.

Phase 1: Vision & Planning

Refine your vision, research locations, interview architects and builders, establish budget parameters

Now - Year 1

Phase 2: Land Acquisition

Find and purchase land, conduct soil tests and surveys, understand zoning restrictions

Year 1-2

Phase 3: Design Development

Work with architect on detailed plans, select major finishes, complete construction documents

6-9 months

Phase 4: Permitting

Submit plans, navigate approvals, finalize contractor selection

2-4 months

Phase 5: Construction

Site prep → Foundation → Framing → MEP rough-in → Exterior → Interior finishes → Final details

14-20 months

Phase 6: Move-In

Final inspections, punch list completion, landscaping maturation, move in!

1-2 months
